The method

One sentence. Distinct jobs.

Document 0 · source

Numbered corpus

Literary sentences, scientific statements, fairy tales and mathematical theorem statements. Preserve exact source wording and ordinal identification.

↓
JSON procedure 1 · independent reader

Meaningful and structural examination

Read for meaning; identify participants, relational loci and relational work; articulate the whole-sentence achievement and interpretive boundary. Produce one frozen structural reading.

↓
Document 1 · archival record

Original + meaningful account + structural reading

Full record remains preserved for eventual audit and publication. It must not be passed wholesale to the second decoder.

🔒 Firewall 1 · evidence isolation

Produce a clean, verbatim, reading-only handoff. Remove separate original sentences and meaningful interpretations from the decoder-visible document. Keep ordinal linkage outside the decoder context. Purpose: test whether the structural reading alone is sufficient.

Document 2 · restricted input

Frozen structural readings only

One reading per decoder call, in a fresh independent context. Quoted fragments inside the frozen reading are left intact; the reading is not rewritten or paraphrased.

↓
JSON procedure 2 · independent decoder

Recover a unified three-axis coordinate

Use the fifteen primitive definitions and Dimensional Conjunction to identify Transformation Pattern × Completion Topology × Persistence Mode. Compare the strongest rival. Record ambiguity or refusal; never force a cell.

↓
Document 3 · frozen decision

Candidate coordinate + rival + evidential status

Freeze the decision before the decoder can see canonical names, cell examples or intended answers.

🔒 Firewall 2 · canonical-answer isolation

Send only the frozen coordinate to the lookup stage. Purpose: prevent answer leakage and after-the-fact adjustment to fit familiar cell names or examples.

Final decoder · lookup, not reinterpretation

Canonical cell number and short form

Look up the frozen coordinate in the authoritative Fiche Ceathar control table. If unresolved, publish no invented classification.

↓
Document 4 · approved publication

Reassembled card record

Original sentence, meaningful reading, structural reading, structural recovery, three axes, cell number, short form, evidence status and provenance become viewable after the protected work is finished.
